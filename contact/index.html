Community Housing Development Corporation

Creating vibrant communities throughout the Bay Area

For 36 years, CHDC has provided affordable housing for individuals, seniors and families, along with the homeownership guidance and community programs that help neighborhoods thrive.

Buy a home Find housing
  • 36 years serving Bay Area communities
  • ~660 homes developed
  • $183M in affordable housing development

How can we help you today?

  • Homeownership

    Homebuyer workshops, one-on-one counseling and down payment assistance, so you can buy a home and build lasting wealth.

    Start your path

  • Find Housing

    Affordable housing for individuals, seniors and families.

    Explore housing

  • Community Programs

    Senior lunches, a food pantry, job and literacy support, neighborhood clean-ups and Earth Day in North Richmond.

    See programs

  • Our Work

    A Black-led, regional affordable housing developer of single-family homes, rental housing and mixed-use communities.
