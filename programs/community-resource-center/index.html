Community Resource Center

Employment, health and human services, and literacy support for North Richmond residents.

Community Resource Center
1540 Fred Jackson Way, Richmond, CA
Monday–Friday, 9 am–2 pm
(510) 255-7805 or (510) 255-7799
ADA accessible

Employment

Support with EDD services, Richmond Works, résumé help, job placement and re-entry.

Health and human services

Connections to the Food Bank, WIC and Medi-Cal eligibility.

Literacy

English as a Second Language (ESL), LEAP and GED support.
