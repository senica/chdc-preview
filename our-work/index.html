Our Work

A Black-led, regional affordable housing developer building homes and community across the Bay Area.

CHDC develops single-family homes, multi-unit rental housing and mixed-use developments in Alameda, Contra Costa and Solano counties, with an emphasis on serving households earning less than 80% of area median income. Our approach pairs thoughtful design and construction with real engagement from the neighbors who live there.
