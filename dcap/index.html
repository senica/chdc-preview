What DCAP is

The Driving Clean Assistance Program (DCAP), offered in partnership with the California Air Resources Board (CARB), helps income-qualified residents of eligible communities in 12 California counties move to cleaner vehicles.

DCAP is separate from CHDC’s housing and homeownership services. The program opened in 12 counties in 2024.

Frequently asked questions

Is DCAP part of CHDC’s housing services?

No. DCAP is a separate clean-vehicle program, offered in partnership with the California Air Resources Board (CARB). It is not part of CHDC’s housing or homeownership services.

Where can I check the program’s current status?

Visit drivingcleanca.org (external site) for current eligibility, funding and application information before you apply.
